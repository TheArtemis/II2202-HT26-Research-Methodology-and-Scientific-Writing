\subsection{Research Problem, Aim, and Research Questions}

\guidance{State the research problem, aims, objectives, and the planned research questions precisely. The terms “aim” and “objective” might seem synonymous, but they have different meanings in this context. Aims are high-level descriptions that explain the motivation and intentions, but are not specific or measurable. The research questions are the specific questions you want to find answers to, while the objectives are the actions to be taken in the project to answer them. Good objectives follow the SMART principle, an acronym for Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound. Include hypotheses only when appropriate, as each hypothesis should be a statement that can be proved to either be true or false. Convince yourself that every research question can be answered by the method described in Section 2 and that the scope is realistic within the time frame of the course.}
