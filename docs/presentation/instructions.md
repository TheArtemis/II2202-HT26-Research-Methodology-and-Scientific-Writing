Base your presentation on your very latest version of your final report. As time is limited, it is recommended to focus on the problem, method, results, and conclusions, while not spending too much time on covering background material. Only include ethics and sustainability aspects in your presentation if they played an important role in your project.

Suggested slides (do not forget to number them):

Title slide: Project title[:subtitle], Name of students who conducted the project, Date of the oral presentation (1 slide)
Problem Statement, Why the problem is important to solve, and your Goals, problem context, [hypothesis], delimitations, ... (1 slide)
Background and Related Work (b slides)
Research Method used to solve the problem (m slides)
Results and Analysis (r slides)
Conclusion (1 slide)
Future Work (1 slide)
[Final slide - to solicit questions (1 slide)]
I would suggest b ≤ 2, m ≤ 2, r ≤ 2, in any case, b+m+r ≤ 6.

Note that Conclusions and Future Work can be combined on one slide.
